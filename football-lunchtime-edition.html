🐯 Derby Day editorial

🐯 Young Kenny

Ready to become a derby-day hero?

Johnny Kenny arrives at Deepdale carrying something this PNE side desperately needs: the instinct to turn chances into goals. He is quick, direct and happiest attacking the space behind a defence. His prolific spell with Shamrock Rovers — including five goals in six Conference League matches — showed that he can deliver when the occasion grows.

Kenny is not being asked to rescue an entire season on his own. He represents something more encouraging: the beginning of the North End side Paul Heckingbottom intended to build, rather than the incomplete version forced through a difficult pre-season.

A home derby would be quite an introduction. Whether starting or emerging from the bench, Kenny brings fresh legs, movement and the possibility that one of those PNE chances finally falls to a natural finisher. If North End are about to turn the corner, Young Kenny might be the man who points them in the right direction.
