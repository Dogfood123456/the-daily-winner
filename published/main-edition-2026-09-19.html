Ayr We Go Again - Maybe!

Gold Cup Day has taken another dramatic turn, with Ayr facing a second inspection at 1.45pm after torrential rain left standing water on the track. The first race has already been pushed back to 2.20pm, so for now the entire card comes with one fairly important caveat: they still need the green light.

If racing does go ahead, conditions will be properly testing — and that has forced a fresh look at the whole Ayr card. Proven mudlarks move up the pecking order, confidence comes down in a few places, and the Gold Cup has changed shape again.
