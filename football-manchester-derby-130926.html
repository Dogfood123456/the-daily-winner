🐝 Matchday editorial

🐝

The bee has split loyalties

Manchester's worker bee belongs to the whole city, but for ninety minutes even the insects need to pick a side. Red remembers January. Blue points to the table. United have the volume, the home record and Bruno Fernandes; City have the cleaner form line and the most destructive centre-forward in the country.

This does not feel like a derby for cautious possession and polite applause. United have scored seven and conceded six in three league games. City have scored seven of their own. Both sides have registered 19 shots on target. The evidence keeps leading back to the same conclusion: goals, momentum swings and a city arguing with itself until the final whistle.

One city · Two empires · Absolutely no quiet afternoon
