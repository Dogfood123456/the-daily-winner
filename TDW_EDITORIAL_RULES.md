# The Daily Winner — Editorial Rules

## Reader-facing rule: NO MACHINERY

The published/draft Main Edition must never expose internal data-source or model machinery to readers.

Do not mention:
- Proform
- UR1
- Andy H
- EvP as a source label
- internal ranking/model language
- named external tipsters as justification for TDW selections
- "speed figure" / "model" / "cross-check" style engine-room wording in reader-facing tip copy

Internal data may be used to make selections, but the published explanation should say what the horse has done, why today suits, and why the price is interesting.

Examples of good reader-facing copy:
- "Ran a strong race here recently and returns under similar conditions."
- "Has been holding his form well and this trip/ground should suit."
- "Comes here thriving and still looks fairly priced."

Examples to avoid:
- "UR1 #1 and top recent Andy H figure."
- "Proform ranks this horse first."
- "EvP says 4/1."
- "Our model gives extra weight to a 50-day same-surface figure."

## Rankings and prices

- Build the Top 3 price-blind internally.
- Reader-facing legend should simply say rankings were formed before prices.
- Show normal guide/early prices only; do not expose the pricing source or internal labels.

## Editorial sources

Racing Post and other outside material may be used for news, quotes, going, market context and factual background. Do not present another publication's tipster selections as TDW analysis.

## Main tips

Use exactly three headline selections unless explicitly changed:
- NAP
- NB
- Editor's Pick

Explain each in plain racing language, not data-source language.

## Workflow

Draft first. Inspect and tidy. Publish only after explicit approval.
