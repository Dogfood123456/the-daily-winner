🌧️ Today's Ground Watch

THE GROUND MOVED THE GOALPOSTS

Beverley Heavy · Goodwood Soft · Yarmouth Soft · Cork Yielding

The overnight rain has changed the complexion of today's cards. Rather than placing too much faith in quick-ground form, we have given extra weight to horses who have already shown they can produce their best when the turf has some give in it.

Beverley is the most extreme test, with Heavy ground making proven stamina and balance especially important. Goodwood and Yarmouth are Soft, while Cork has eased to Yielding.

That does not mean throwing recent form out of the window. It simply means a strong recent run on fast ground is not automatically more relevant than a slightly older performance achieved under conditions much closer to today's.

The result is a card with a few different names rising to prominence — and a Sunday where checking the going before betting remains especially important.

🗼 The Road to Paris

THE ROAD TO PARIS

The Daily Winner's early look at the 2026 Prix de l'Arc de Triomphe

Just when we thought Constitution Hill's extraordinary Flat adventure could not become more interesting, it has given us a route into another story. Dual Group 1 winner Bay City Roller is expected to meet Constitution Hill in Saturday's September Stakes at Kempton — but Kempton is only the stepping stone. The overriding objective is Paris on 4 October.

At the centre of the Arc picture is Daryz, last year's winner and the horse the market expects everyone else to beat. Behind him sits an increasingly fascinating group including Maltese Cross, Kalpana, Item, Bay City Roller, Thundering On and last year's runner-up Minnie Hauk.

ARC MARKET WATCH
Daryz ~3/1 · Maltese Cross ~6/1 · Kalpana ~7/1–8/1 · Item ~12/1 · Bay City Roller ~12/1–16/1 · Thundering On ~14/1 · Minnie Hauk ~16/1–20/1

The ground may become one of the defining threads. Bay City Roller's connections regard ease underfoot as important and have chosen Kempton rather than an expected good-ground Prix Foy. That makes every Longchamp weather update increasingly relevant as October approaches.

🩺 Yesterday's Cheerful Autopsy

ONE STILL RUNNING!

Alfred Wallace saves Saturday's blushes — and does it in style.

Saturday was more hard hat than party hat, but Alfred Wallace made sure we didn't leave empty-handed. Our 3.30 Sandown Value selection won the Solario Stakes, getting home by half a length after the ground eased to Good to Soft. The 7/1 we published disappeared before the off and he returned 11/4 — not bad evidence that the original price was worth taking.

Pierre Royal gave the Tips column its best result, finishing third in the Irish Cambridgeshire. Pivotal Attack finished eighth, while Amazonian Dream was fifth at Windsor. On the Value side Ziata finished sixth at Beverley and Addison Grey was a non-runner.

The useful lesson is not to pretend Saturday was better than it was. The winner came from the Value Desk, the main Tips didn't land one, and the changing ground caused plenty of late complications. But Alfred Wallace was a proper result: identified at a much bigger price than SP and good enough to win a Group 3.
