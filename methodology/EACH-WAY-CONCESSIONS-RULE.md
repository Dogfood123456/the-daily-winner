# THE DAILY WINNER — EACH-WAY & BOOKMAKER CONCESSIONS RULE

**Status:** Permanent pricing rule / Main Edition methodology addendum  
**Date added:** 2026-08-30

## Core principle

Bookmaker concessions can influence **HOW we bet a selection — never WHICH horse we select**.

The predictive ranking, Top 3 Every Race and eligibility for Top 3 Tips/Value must be established by the normal racing evidence. Extra places, enhanced each-way terms and other bookmaker concessions must never promote a horse in the predictive rankings.

## Each-way review

During the post-freeze pricing stage:

- Any headline selection at roughly **6/1 or bigger** should trigger an **each-way review**, not an automatic each-way bet.
- Check the actual bookmaker each-way terms and specifically look for **extra-place concessions**.
- Compare the enhanced terms with the normal place terms for the race.
- Give meaningful credit to unusually generous concessions such as 4, 5 or 6 places where they materially improve the betting proposition.
- Record the actual recommended bet as **WIN** or **EW** before publication.
- If EW is recommended, publish the relevant enhanced place terms where useful, e.g. **15/2 EW — 5 places**.

## Exposure discipline

Each-way betting doubles the stake for the same nominal unit, so it is not free insurance.

Do **not** automatically bet each-way merely because a horse is 6/1, 8/1 or bigger. Ask whether the place terms are sufficiently attractive to justify splitting the stake. Weak standard terms may make a straight win bet preferable; strong extra-place concessions can make EW materially more attractive.

The Value Desk should therefore answer two separate questions:

1. **Is the horse value at the available price?**
2. **Are the each-way terms/concessions good enough to make splitting the stake worthwhile?**

## Autopsy

Score the bet exactly as it was published. If a selection was advised EW with enhanced places, judge the result using those published terms rather than standard race terms or hindsight.

Example from 30 August 2026: Beau Army was available at 15/2 in an extra-place race paying **5 places**. Finishing fourth therefore returned the place part. This illustrates why concessions must be captured during pricing rather than reconstructed after the race.
