🗓️ Weekly Research Desk

What earns attention?

A performance that reaches or approaches the standard we expect to matter at the Festival; evidence that repeats rather than flashes once; credible information about the intended target; and finally a price that still compensates us for the months of uncertainty between now and Cheltenham.
