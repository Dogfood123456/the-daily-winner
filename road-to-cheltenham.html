🗓️ Weekly Research Desk

What earns attention?

A performance that reaches or approaches Festival standard; evidence that repeats rather than flashes once; credible target information; and a price that compensates for uncertainty. For private Anticipation Bets, the extra test is asymmetry: is the next plausible piece of evidence much more likely to destroy today's price than improve it?
