THE ROAD TO THE 250th ST LEGER

Derby hero Christmas Day heads the Classic conversation as Doncaster week begins

The oldest Classic reaches a landmark running this weekend. Christmas Day, Enceladus and Pierre Bonnard give the market three very different routes to Doncaster — and the final pieces are beginning to fall into place.

ST LEGER COUNTDOWN · DONCASTER

CHRISTMAS DAY HAS THE CLASSIC CREDENTIALS

Christmas Day brings the strongest established Classic form into the St Leger picture after winning the Derby and finishing second in the Irish Derby. His Great Voltigeur third need not be viewed as a fatal blow: plenty of Leger winners have arrived at Doncaster after being beaten in York's traditional trial.

Pierre Bonnard finished ahead of him at York and represents the obvious improving rival, while Enceladus has taken a very different route after progressing from handicap company. Highwayman and Hatteen sit among the more interesting alternatives if the market leaders leave the door open.

EARLY TDW VIEW · Christmas Day's York defeat does not erase his Derby and Irish Derby form. We will follow declarations, ground and the market through the week before making a final St Leger call.
