Doncaster's St Leger Festival rolls into Friday with the staying stars in the Doncaster Cup

Good-to-soft ground dominates Friday's British Flat cards, with softer patches at Chester and on Sandown's sprint course. Recent form achieved with some cut therefore carries extra weight again today.

🏆 DONCASTER CUP DAY

The St Leger Festival moves into its second day with the Doncaster Cup at 3.00.
