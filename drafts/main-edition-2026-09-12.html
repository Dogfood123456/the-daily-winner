250 YEARS OF THE ST LEGER

History at Doncaster as the world's oldest Classic celebrates its 250th running

Christmas Day arrives attempting to add the St Leger to his Derby crown, but this is no coronation. The Daily Winner's supplied speed evidence makes the Classic one of the most intriguing races of the entire Saturday card.

🏆 THE 250th ST LEGER

Two and a half centuries after the race was first staged, Doncaster hosts a genuine piece of racing history. Derby winner Christmas Day bids for a double last completed by Reference Point in 1987. Our price-blind speed work, however, refuses to simply follow the script: Pierre Bonnard heads our three, ahead of Hatteen and Rebel Rocker. Christmas Day and Enceladus remain obvious dangers in a Classic where stamina and tactics will matter every bit as much as reputation.
