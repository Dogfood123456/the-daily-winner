Binhareer hardens to 4/1 — and Saffie sneaks into the Gold Cup on Twilight Calls.

The final 25-runner Ayr Gold Cup field is taking shape around a clear market leader. Binhareer is now 4/1, ahead of Far Above Dream at 8/1, with Tuco Salamanca and Dance In The Storm at 10/1.

The late drama came at the cut line. Twilight Calls grabbed the final place in the field after four withdrawals and will be ridden by Saffie Osborne, fresh from her historic St Leger success. He is around 11/1, just ahead of a deep pack headed by Evening Saigon and a cluster of 16/1 shots including Fine Interview, Red Spells Danger, Run Boy Run, Stratusnine, Two Tribes and Wiltshire.

We are not forcing a Gold Cup selection today. The field is finally set; now we wait for the full Saturday picture before committing.
