THE ROAD HAS REACHED DONCASTER

St Leger Festival begins — and the countdown to the historic 250th running enters its final days

Four days of racing begin on Town Moor today, with the Park Hill and May Hill taking centre stage before Saturday's landmark St Leger. The ground has eased to good to soft, good in places — another reminder that September is beginning to change the questions.

DONCASTER OPENS THE FESTIVAL

The St Leger story moves from preview to raceweek today. The Park Hill brings established staying fillies and mares together at 3.00, while the May Hill at 3.35 offers an early look at some of the fillies who may shape next season. Saturday's 250th St Leger remains the destination, but today's card is the first proper taste of the festival.

Ground Watch: Doncaster is good to soft, good in places, with light rain around. That matters: recent figures earned with some ease underfoot receive extra respect today rather than blindly chasing the single highest summer number.

🏖️ Racing on the Beach!

A UNIQUE DAY AT LAYTOWN

There aren't many afternoons in the calendar quite like this one. For a few hours the traditional racecourse disappears and the strand on the County Meath coast becomes the track, with horses racing on the beach in front of a packed seaside crowd.

It is also a useful warning against treating every Flat meeting as identical. Laytown's beach surface is its own test, so today's rankings deserve a little more caution than a conventional turf or Polytrack card. Reponse Finale at 6.50 and Valiant Force at 7.20 are particularly interesting on the available evidence.
