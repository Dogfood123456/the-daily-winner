📰 The Big Saturday Story

The road forks at Kempton

PARIS OR MELBOURNE?

There are Group 3s, and then there are races that feel like junctions. Saturday's September Stakes at Kempton could point several very different careers in completely different directions.

Constitution Hill arrives as the great hurdler turned Flat curiosity, unbeaten in two starts on the level but now stepping from novice company into a race containing genuine Group performers. His official Flat mark of 101 leaves him with something to find, and Nicky Henderson has made no attempt to disguise the scale of the test. The point of Saturday is to discover exactly where they stand.

At the other end of the road are horses with Paris on their minds. Gethin, runner-up to Ombudsman before finishing fifth in the Eclipse, is using Kempton as an Arc test. Dual Group 1 winner Bay City Roller, brilliant in the Coronation Cup, is another possible Arc-bound heavyweight. Group 1 winner Tornado Alert adds still more quality to a 14-strong entry.

For Constitution Hill, the destination could instead be much farther away. The Melbourne Cup remains the extraordinary dream. Saturday is his third Flat start and a chance to show whether that dream belongs in serious company rather than the realms of racing fantasy.

One race. Two continents. And by Saturday afternoon the map may look very different.
