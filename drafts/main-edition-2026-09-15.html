YARMOUTH · REDCAR · WOLVERHAMPTON · PUNCHESTOWN · 31 FLAT RACES · TUESDAY MAIN EDITION

Three days, three sprint cups — Ayr's biggest week is taking shape.

Scotland's biggest Flat meeting builds through the week, with Friday's Bronze Cup setting the sprint story rolling before Saturday's Silver Cup and the Ayr Gold Cup itself complete the trilogy.

Richard Spencer could have a strong hand. Twilight Calls is particularly interesting if he gets into the Gold Cup, while Run Boy Run, Two Tribes and Candy give the yard further strength in depth. Last year's Gold Cup winner Run Boy Run and Silver Cup winner Candy are both back in the calculations.

The supporting programme has plenty of depth too. Friday also features the Harry Rosebery Stakes and the Arran Scottish Sprint Fillies' Stakes, while Saturday brings the Firth of Clyde Stakes and the Doonside Cup. The final shape of the three sprint handicaps will become clearer as declarations and the Gold Cup draw fall into place.
