Galopin Des Champs · 2016–2026

A CHAMPION TO THE END

Galopin Des Champs has died at the age of ten after suffering a severe injury in his field. Vets attended immediately, but X-rays showed damage that could not be repaired.

He had retired only five days earlier, with a second career in eventing planned under Isabelle Walsh. Instead, racing is mourning a horse who won 15 of his 26 starts, collected two Cheltenham Gold Cups and became the defining staying chaser of his generation.

Numbers tell only part of the story. His brilliance on the racecourse came with a gentle nature away from it, and the memories belong not just to Willie Mullins, Paul Townend and everyone at Closutton, but to every racing fan who felt the surge when he swept into contention.

Goodnight, champ. Thank you for everything.
