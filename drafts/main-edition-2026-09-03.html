THE FINAL TURN BEFORE THE ARC

Four weeks before the Prix de l’Arc de Triomphe, the Prix Niel, Prix Foy and Prix Vermeille offer one last chance to test the course, sharpen the form and force a way into Europe’s greatest Flat race. Each winner receives an automatic Arc place—and a horse not already entered earns a free supplementary entry worth €120,000.

Benvenuto Cellini arrives after winning the Irish Derby and finishing third against older horses in the King George. Daryz, last year’s Arc hero, is the class standard in the Foy, although his connections make no secret that October—not Sunday—is the day that matters most. The Vermeille looks tighter: Yorkshire Oaks runner-up Sparan Nua is respected, but Sunly’s close third over the Arc distance in the Grand Prix de Saint-Cloud earns our narrow preference.
