📰 Sunday Special

WE TOOK ON THE TIPSTERS — AND WON

Nine winners from 20 like-for-like races put The Daily Winner on top of Sunday's comparison.

Sunday presented us with a problem before the first race had even started. The rain had changed the questions. Beverley was Heavy, Goodwood and Yarmouth were Soft and Cork was Yielding, so blindly favouring the newest piece of fast-ground form would have missed the point.

We made the call before racing to give extra respect to performances achieved under conditions that actually resembled the ones the horses were about to face. Recent form remained important; relevance mattered more when the ground had moved dramatically.

On the 20 Beverley, Goodwood and Yarmouth races where we could make a direct like-for-like comparison, our first selection won nine times — 45%. RP Ratings found seven; RP Postdata six; Spotlight three; Proform Power Ratings two; Topspeed none. The graphic tells the story rather more efficiently than we can.

Across the entire 27-race board, our No.1 won ten times and the winner was among our Top 3 in 16 races. There were also three complete Top-3 sweeps — Beverley 2.15, Goodwood 2.00 and Cork 4.05 — with our first choice winning each.

That does not mean we tear up the normal preference for strong recent evidence. It means Sunday's lesson was simpler: when conditions change radically, a performance achieved in genuinely comparable conditions can tell us more than a newer run on completely different ground.

One great Sunday doesn't prove a system. It does, however, earn it the right to be taken seriously.
