The Daily Winner briefly considers changing its name to The Daily Third.

There are bad days, and then there are days with an almost artistic commitment to finishing third. Sovereign View, Remi Mae and John Harrison — yesterday’s entire Top 3 Tips — all occupied the final step of the podium. Three tips. Three thirds. No winners. You almost have to admire the consistency.

The Value Desk at least retained a little dignity: Star Harbour finished second at 15/2. The Green Mile was seventh at 13/2, while our adventurous Goldilocks Cen place-market experiment finished seventh at a rather spectacular 200/1 SP. That one can be quietly returned to the enormous racing filing cabinet marked “Well, that didn’t work.”
