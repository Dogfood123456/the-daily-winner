🏇 From the Editor's Desk

CHANGE OF PLAN: KEMPTON IT IS

No Windsor for Constitution Hill — but the next test could be even more revealing.

Yesterday we told you Constitution Hill could be heading to Windsor on Saturday. Well… scratch that.

Nicky Henderson has decided not to run him at Windsor, with the ground unlikely to be suitable. Instead, the plan is to wait another week and head back to Kempton for the Group 3 September Stakes.

That means a tougher assignment than the Listed race at Windsor — but also a return to a track and surface he already knows.

No Windsor. One more week's wait. Bigger test.
