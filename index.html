🏇 From the Editor's Desk

BILLY THE KID IS THE REAL DEAL

Twenty years old, leading the title race — and we reckon this is only the beginning.

There are promising young jockeys, and then there are the ones who look like they belong at the very top. We reckon Billy Loughnane is firmly in the second camp.

Still only 20, he's already riding with the confidence, judgement and composure of somebody considerably further into his career. Now Tony Hind — the leading jockeys' agent who helped guide Ryan Moore to three titles — has compared Billy's rise with Moore's.

The jockeys' title would be some achievement, but we're interested in the bigger picture.

We think Billy is the real deal — and we'll be surprised if we're not watching him win the very biggest races for years to come.
