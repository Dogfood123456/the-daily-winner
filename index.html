FOUR STAND BETWEEN HILL AND MELBOURNE

Constitution Hill and Billy Loughnane face four rivals in the September Stakes. Bay City Roller’s absence has eased the field, but Gethin and Tornado Alert ensure this is no gentle assignment for the former Champion Hurdler.

He has always had the engine; Saturday is about discovering how far it will take him on the Flat. A win would open extraordinary doors. A defeat would not diminish the fascination.

THE MELBOURNE DREAM

For now, it is beautifully simple: finish in the first three today and the Melbourne Cup dream stays alive. The September Stakes is a Group 3 over 2,413 metres, which is enough to meet the relevant-performance requirement for the Cup ballot.

There will be weights, acceptances and travel hurdles to negotiate later. First, Constitution Hill has to show that the extraordinary idea belongs in serious staying company.
