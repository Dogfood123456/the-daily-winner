💡 Is Each-Way Always the Best Way?

Sometimes the two halves are cheaper bought separately.

Goldilocks Cen is around 66/1 to win, but the separate place-only market has offered 30/1 just to place.

With this race paying ¼ the odds each-way, the place part of a 66/1 each-way bet is effectively only 16.5/1. The standalone place price is almost twice as big.

£10 place at effective E/W terms (16.5/1): £175 return
£10 place at 30/1 place-only: £310 return

The lesson: price the bet as well as the horse.
