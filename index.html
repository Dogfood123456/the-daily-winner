🏇 From the Editor's Desk

FROM CHAMPION HURDLES TO WINDSOR ON A SATURDAY NIGHT

Constitution Hill's Flat adventure could be about to get considerably more serious.

Constitution Hill is among 19 entries for Saturday's August Stakes at Windsor — and if anyone imagined this would be a gentle Listed-race stroll, somebody appears to have forgotten to tell the opposition.

Possible rivals include White Birch, conqueror of Auguste Rodin in the 2024 Tattersalls Gold Cup; Canadian International winner Silawi; last year's August Stakes winner Arabian Crown; the evergreen Al Aasy; and Willie Mullins' Gaucher. The King and Queen could even be represented by Warrant Holder.

That's what makes it fascinating. Constitution Hill doesn't need to prove he's a great racehorse. We already know that. But seeing one of the great jumps horses trying to reinvent himself against proper Flat horses on their own territory is wonderfully unusual.

If he turns up, we'll be watching. And presumably Willie Mullins will be too.

🩺 Yesterday's Cheerful Autopsy

BACK TO BUSINESS — WITH MIXED RESULTS

Runninsonofagun got the job done — and Liberate joined the party.

Runninsonofagun WON at Musselburgh, rewarding our faith in a horse who had kept performing despite the handicapper's repeated attempts to load the carriage. Liberate also WON at Brighton, while Top Tip South Shore went desperately close in second.

Percival finished fifth, Torbados fourth and our 18/1 swing Beyond The Bar finished ninth. So the Value Desk didn't exactly require extra shelving, but two winners and a runner-up among the horses we spent most time arguing about is a perfectly respectable Monday.

Most importantly, the handicapper was heard, respected and — on a couple of occasions — politely ignored.
