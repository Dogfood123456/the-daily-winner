🏆 Title Watch

THE CHAMPION STRIKES BACK?

Billy Loughnane 106 — Oisin Murphy 91

Billy Loughnane made hay while the champion was away yesterday and the gap now stands at fifteen. Oisin Murphy is back on British soil today, though, and heads to Windsor with opportunities to begin the reply while Billy goes to Brighton.

BILLY LOUGHNANE106Championship leader

The interesting part for us is that their bookings wander into races we already care about. Murphy partners Cape Fear in the 6.18 Windsor, where our preference is Percival, while Loughnane rides Kondratiev Wave in Brighton's 5.00.

We're not backing jockeys instead of horses. But when a horse already makes the shortlist, knowing who's riding — and what they're riding for — certainly doesn't hurt.

🩺 Yesterday's Cheerful Autopsy

THE TRAIN HAS TERMINATED.

Please take all belongings with you.

We promised to stay aboard Arnaz until the winning run ended. It ended. Giant won the 3.50 Brighton, Arnaz finished sixth and our value companion Dion Baker came home ninth. No complaints: we'd watched Arnaz win twice, stayed aboard for the hat-trick attempt and now have our answer. Tickets, please.

The headline tips were more respectable than profitable: Highland Harvey finished third and Soul Love second. Value pick Roeillaun finished third, while Diamond Exchange was eighth. Nobody troubled the cashier sufficiently for him to learn our names.

Fortunately, the racecard was having a considerably better afternoon...
