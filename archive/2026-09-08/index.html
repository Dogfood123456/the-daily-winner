Tuesday's Big Story

The Hill Gets Steeper

Beaten at Kempton. Raised 7lb. Australia gone — but Constitution Hill’s Flat adventure is only getting interesting.

Constitution Hill

A DEFEAT THAT EARNED MORE RESPECT

Constitution Hill’s Melbourne Cup dream is over after his fourth in the September Stakes — but the handicapper has delivered the strangest possible verdict on his first Flat defeat: up 7lb to 108.

Five-and-three-quarter lengths behind Pride Of Arras, he had faced proven Group horses for the first time. The assessor read it as a step forward, not a backward one. That is an awkward mark for the usual handicaps, but Nicky Henderson says he has come out of Kempton in great form and believes a return to turf over a mile and a half to a mile and six will suit.

Australia has gone. The dream has not. The Hill has got steeper — and the next chapter may be the most intriguing yet.

WHAT NEXT?
Henderson will consider longer Flat races and a turf return. A 108 mark makes the standard 0–105 handicap route difficult, but higher-grade autumn handicaps and conditions races remain live possibilities.
