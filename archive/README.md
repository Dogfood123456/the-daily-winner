# The Daily Winner — Edition Archive

This directory is the permanent archive of published editions.

## Structure

- `archive/main/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.html`
- `archive/evening/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.html`
- `archive/tipster-tracker/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.html`

## Archive rule

Before a live edition is replaced, preserve the edition that readers actually saw under its dated archive path. Archived editions are historical records and should not be silently rewritten after publication.

If a genuine correction is required, preserve the original record and document the correction rather than rewriting history.

The archive exists so historical selections, prices, each-way terms, Top 3 rankings, editorial and autopsy claims can be searched and verified later.
